# 5-Second Cognitive Orientation Rubric

When an AI assistant refers a user to a webpage, the visitor must understand within 5 seconds:
1. **Who is the entity/brand?** (Clear brand name in header/title).
2. **What problem does it solve?** (Concise value proposition in H1 within top 1,000 characters).
3. **What is the next action?** (Clear visible call to action, navigation, or search).

If an AI referral lands on a page with generic buzzwords ("Transform your future with synergy"), missing H1, or confusing multi-product ambiguity, the visitor will bounce back to the AI assistant.
